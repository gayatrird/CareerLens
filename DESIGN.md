## Brand & Visual Philosophy
CareerLens is an AI-powered career intelligence platform designed with precision, futuristic minimalism, and clear data visualization. The aesthetic blends deep slate surfaces with luminous blue focal accents and intuitive status indicators.

## Color Palette
- **Foundational Background**: `#09090B` (Deep Void) with subtle 40px grid and atmospheric radial glows.
- **Surface Elevation**: `#171A20` and `#18181B` with 1px border `#2D2F36` or `#27272A`.
- **Primary Brand Accent**: `#5B8CFF` / `#4F7DF3` (Career Intelligence Blue).
- **Evaluation Status**:
  - High Match / Strong Fit: `#22C55E` (Emerald)
  - Partial Match / Review: `#F59E0B` (Amber)
  - Gaps / Low Alignment: `#EF4444` (Coral Red)
  - Intelligence / Insight: `#8B5CF6` (Violet)

## Typography
Clean typography using `Inter` with modern sans weights (400, 500, 600, 700, 900) across display, body, and labelcaps utilities.
